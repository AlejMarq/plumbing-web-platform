Meet Our Family

A Family-Owned Plumbing Business You Can Trust

At The Go-To Plumber, we believe every customer deserves honest communication, dependable workmanship, and personalized service. As a family-owned business serving North Georgia, we're committed to treating every home and business with the same care and respect we'd expect for our own.

Meet Erick

Owner & Lead Plumber

Erick is the owner and lead plumber of The Go-To Plumber. With over 10 years of proven plumbing experience, he works directly with homeowners and businesses throughout North Georgia, providing dependable plumbing solutions backed by honest pricing and quality workmanship.

From leak repairs and drain cleaning to remodeling, water heater installations, new construction, and commercial plumbing, Erick takes pride in doing the job right and making sure every customer feels confident in the work being performed.

Meet Karen

Office Manager

Karen keeps the business running behind the scenes. She manages scheduling, customer communication, estimates, invoices, and helps ensure every customer has a smooth experience from the first phone call to the completion of the job.

While Erick is out serving customers in the field, Karen keeps everything organized so projects stay on schedule and customers always know what to expect.
